# Budget Process

The GSS annual budget covers one fiscal year (June 1–May 31). The budget
preparation process runs **February through May**, with the incoming executive
team working on the budget for their term during the transition period.

---

## The Road to the Budget

### Step 1: Update Budget Categories
*Responsible: Financial Officer & Accountant*

Before drafting the budget, review and update the chart of accounts with the Accountant. Two things to check:

- **Account usage:** Review the *Chart of Accounts – Accounts* sheet in the outgoing budget to identify lines that were unused, misused, or missing.
- **Reporting needs:** Cross-reference with the updated category explanations in the [Finance and Accounting Guide](https://ubcgss.github.io/finance_accounting_guide/zoho/budget-categories/).

If changes are needed, log them in the *Chart of Accounts – Change Log* sheet of the **new budget** (not the ongoing one). Mark any superseded lines as such. The *Accounts* sheet will update automatically, and you can then begin planning.

## February–May Timeline

(Source: HF Policy Appendix II, Financial Budget Guideline)

### February
- Accountant, HFC, GM, and FO review, discuss, and alter (if needed) the
  existing budget notes.
- GM and FO send out a **new budget timeline** to all executives.
- GM and FO meet outgoing executives for budget feedback and suggestions for
  the next year's budget.
- GM and FO send out a **new budget line template** to all departments.
- FO participates in the election's all-candidate meeting.

### March
- FO and GM meet elected (incoming) executives to ascertain their preliminary
  goals and how budgetary allocations would be affected.

### April
- Elected executives provide **first departmental draft budgets** to HFC
  (mid-April).
- HFC commences review of executive budgets.
- Elected executives provide **second departmental draft budgets** to HFC by
  end of April.

### May
- HFC reviews and discusses the second departmental draft budgets.
- Executives make recommended changes.
- FO runs the [Budget Integrity Review](#budget-integrity-review) on the final
  draft and fixes or explains every finding before HFC approval.
- HFC approves the final master budget **at least one week before** the May
  Council meeting.
- FO presents the final master budget at the May Council meeting.
- Master budget is approved at the May Council meeting.

## Five-Department Structure

The GSS budget is a master budget comprising five departmental sub-budgets:

| Code | Department | Responsible executive | Budget type |
|------|-----------|----------------------|------------|
| **10-CORP** | Corporate | President, GM, FO | Revenue + expenditure |
| **11-PRES** | President | President | Expenditure |
| **12-VPUAA** | VP University and Academic Affairs | VP UAA | Expenditure |
| **13-VPEXT** | VP External Relations | VP External | Expenditure |
| **14-VPSTD** | VP Students | VP Students | Revenue + expenditure |

**10-CORP major revenues:** Membership fees, Sauder lease charges, Koerner's
Pub space rental charges, bookings space rental charges.

**10-CORP major expenses:** Staff salaries and benefits, executives'
honoraria, professional service charges, commercial rent to UBC, marketing
costs.

**14-VPSTD major revenues:** Programs, events, orientation sales.

## Budget Reallocation

During the fiscal year, reallocations between budget lines may be made by
the FO in consultation with the Executives and the HFC. Such redistributions
**must be reported to Council**.

## Budget Integrity Review

The review checks that the **budget itself** is built correctly: every number
traceable to a source, calculated rather than copied, and adding up. It is not
the monthly budget vs. actuals review.

**When:** on the final draft before HFC approval, and again after every revision
of the master during the year.

**How:** run the [Budget Check](../../tools/budget-check/) on a saved copy of the
master, then work through the list below. Items 1–5 are in the tool's report;
items 6 and 7 are by hand. Save the report and your notes in the budget task
folder in Dropbox.

1. **Parameters are sourced and valid for the whole year.** Each contract
   amount, rate, and payment date on the Assumptions tab names its source
   document and clause and covers June 1 to May 31. A value that changes during
   the year (such as CRA payroll rates on January 1) has one row per period, and
   each month uses the row that covers it. Lapsed parameters are
   renewed from the contract, an HFC decision, or the previous value inflated by
   CPI. *(Tool: Parameters)*
2. **Lines are calculated, not rolled forward.** A line with its own
   calculation tab (FT Staff, Honoraria, WL Supporting, and others) links to it.
   Lines rolled forward from last year are small, have no better basis, and say
   so in the Notes column. *(Tool: Rolled-forward formulas)*
3. **No hard-coded numbers.** Rates and amounts live on the Assumptions tab and
   formulas link to them. No numbers inside formulas, no typed values among
   formulas, and no links to other workbooks. *(Tool: Hard-coded numbers,
   Internal consistency)*
4. **The workbook adds up.** Each line's annual amount equals its months,
   every code is in the Chart of Accounts, and Consolidated equals the
   department tabs. *(Tool: Internal consistency)*
5. **Required costs have a line.** Employer CPP/EI, WorkSafeBC, the audit,
   insurance, the lease, and bank fees each have a budget. *(Tool: Required
   lines)*
6. **Months match the payment calendar.** Each line's monthly spread follows
   when the money moves (for example UBC fee remittances in June, October, and
   February), not an even 1/12 when the payments are lumpy. Use the Calendar
   rows on the Assumptions tab.
7. **Every change is logged and approved.** Each revision has an entry in the
   change log with its effect on the net. Reallocations are reported to Council.

{: .warning }
> **Needs confirmation:** whether a revision that *increases* the budgeted
> deficit, rather than moving money between lines, needs a Council motion.
> Confirm with HFC before relying on this page.

## Budgeting Principles

1. **Provide realistic estimates.** Budget padding (underestimating revenue or
   overestimating costs) is not permitted. Each executive is accountable for
   accurate projections and must explain variances when comparing actuals to
   budget.

2. **Standardize data and processes.** Use the standard template provided by
   the GM and Accountant. The GSS uses Microsoft Excel for budgeting.

3. **Accountability and accessibility.** Each department head must be prepared
   to explain variances between actuals and budget at every monthly review.
