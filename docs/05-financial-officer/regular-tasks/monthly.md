# Monthly Tasks

The FO's recurring monthly cycle follows the credit card statement period
and the Council meeting schedule. Council meets on the **3rd Thursday of each
month**, except December where it falls on the **2nd Thursday**.

---

## Download and Share Bank, Credit Card, and Investment Statements

On the **10th of each month**:
1. Download the GSS Vancity statements:
   - **Credit card statement** from [Vancity credit card online banking](https://creditcards.vancity.com/).
   - **Bank account statements** (previous month, one per Vancity account) from [Vancity online banking](https://my.vancity.com/banking/home).
2. Add the statements to the matching folder in the Finance and Accounting SharePoint:
   - [Vancity Banking](https://ubcgss.sharepoint.com/:f:/r/sites/FinanceandAccounting/Shared%20Documents/General/Bank%20and%20Investment%20Statements/Vancity%20Banking%2091577945?d=w39798ac73b464cb4bb5e1203813f6911&csf=1&web=1&e=Twgk4d): bank account statements
   - [Vancity Credit Card](https://ubcgss.sharepoint.com/:f:/r/sites/FinanceandAccounting/Shared%20Documents/General/Bank%20and%20Investment%20Statements/Vancity%20Credit%20Card%200139?d=w952d009b46ea4af8a3326fcc7252f5e7&csf=1&web=1&e=bhbpVg): credit card statements
3. Send the statements to the Accountant and General Manager. *Note: This email is already written so you only have to find it, click on ''send again'' and include the updated statements. The subject line starts with "Vancity Statements yyyy-mm" (update to the current month).*

This provides the Accountant with the official statements needed for
reconciliation. The credit card statement also starts the cardholder
expense upload, which you approve in your
[biweekly review](../biweekly/#credit-card-and-plooto-approvals-zoho-expenses).

### Investment Statements (Quarterly)

GSS investments are held at **RBC PH&N**, which issues statements quarterly.
Each quarter:
1. Download the statement for the quarter ending **March 31, June 30,
   September 30, or December 31**.
2. Save it to the [Investment Statements folder](https://ubcgss.sharepoint.com/:f:/r/sites/FinanceandAccounting/Shared%20Documents/General/Banking,%20Credit%20Card,%20and%20Investment%20Statements/Scotiabank%20Investment?d=w5738858315064b668294c98def60a1ee&csf=1&web=1&e=MHOlN5).
3. **Report the statement to the HFC** at the first HFC meeting after
   quarter-end (usually in April, July, October, and January).

See [Investment Accounts](../../systems/investment/) for the SIP and the
annual review.

## Download and Send CRA Notices to Accountant

Once a month, check the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) account for new notices,
such as assessments, balance owing or arrears notices, and requests for
information.

1. Sign in through the CRA [provincial partner sign-in](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/e-services/digital-services-individuals/account-individuals/provincial-partner.html).
2. Download unread notices and save them to the [CRA Notices folder](https://ubcgss.sharepoint.com/:f:/r/sites/FinanceandAccounting/Shared%20Documents/General/CRA%20Tax%20Documents/CRA%20Notices?d=wb10027664bf447e68b1ff07b67197407&csf=1&web=1&e=M9thrj) in the Finance and Accounting SharePoint.
3. [Email the notices to the Accountant](readdle-spark://compose?recipient=accounting%40gss.ubc.ca&subject=CRA%20Notices%20yyyy-mm&body=Hi%2C%0A%0AAttached%20are%20the%20new%20CRA%20notices%20for%20%5BMONTH%20YEAR%5D%2C%20also%20saved%20in%20the%20CRA%20Notices%20folder%3A%20https%3A%2F%2Fubcgss.sharepoint.com%2F%3Af%3A%2Fr%2Fsites%2FFinanceandAccounting%2FShared%2520Documents%2FGeneral%2FCRA%2520Tax%2520Documents%2FCRA%2520Notices%3Fd%3Dwb10027664bf447e68b1ff07b67197407%26csf%3D1%26web%3D1%26e%3DM9thrj%0A%0AThank%20you%2C%0A%5BYOUR%20NAME%5D).
   The draft opens in [Spark](../../tools/spark/#pre-drafted-email-links) from
   `finance@gss.ubc.ca`; fill in the placeholders and the month in the subject
   line, then attach the new notices and link to the folder before sending.

Do this when you download the statements on the **10th** so both checks happen
on the same day. If there are no new notices, no email is needed.

## Financial Report Review

The monthly financial update is prepared by the Bookkeeper and Accountant
and reviewed by the Accountant and GM.

**Deadline for your review:** The report is due to you by **the Wednesday
before the week of the Council meeting**. You are to review the financial
update report and ask questions or express concerns **by the end of the
following Monday** (i.e., the week of the Council meeting). You then present
the financial update at the **Council meeting**.

If the Wednesday deadline cannot be met in a given month, the Accountant will
advise you and you will agree on a revised timing together.

**What to look for when reviewing:**
- Significant variances between budget and actuals for any department
- Unusual or unbudgeted expenditures
- Any discrepancies or errors flagged by the Bookkeeper

## Monthly Council Presentation and Reporting

Bylaw 8.5.6(a) requires the FO to report to Council on the financial situation
of the Society on a monthly basis. Each Council meeting, you prepare two
things: a brief financial presentation and your work update.

### 1. Financial Presentation

**1.1 Budget vs. Actuals**

Last month's and year-to-date actuals, summarized by department and budget
category. This section is generated automatically from the prior month's data,
so no manual preparation is required beyond reviewing the output for accuracy.

**1.2 Financial Developments**

A brief update on relevant financial matters since the last Council meeting,
which may include:
- Items discussed or decided at the most recent House Finance Committee meeting
- Investment account updates or reports
- Outstanding items carried over from the prior Council meeting

The standard agenda item format is:

```
Title:        Monthly Financial Report - [Month Year]
Responsible:  Financial Officer
Time:         [X] minutes
Background:   The Financial Officer presents an overview of the Society's
              current financial situation, including any budget reallocations
              made since the last Council meeting.
Purpose:      To keep Council informed of the Society's financial position, as
              required under Bylaw 8.5.6(a).
```

### 2. FO Work Update

All executives submit individual work updates to Council each month. For the
FO, this process is largely automated: provided you keep your work log and
meeting notes current in Notion, the data is pulled into a Google Sheet and
formatted for Council automatically.

Select and copy the relevant entries into the agenda before each meeting:
- **Council agenda:** the past four weeks of updates
  ([four-week tab](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1L1m10y5jLQjseSUQkC9rjXJG1Km4j2X1SBZsDNthWVI/edit?gid=1240902386#gid=1240902386))
- **HFC agenda:** the past two weeks of updates
  ([two-week tab](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1L1m10y5jLQjseSUQkC9rjXJG1Km4j2X1SBZsDNthWVI/edit?gid=1067922621#gid=1067922621))

---

The FO and GM must submit the budget and profit and loss statements to the HFC
**within five weeks of the end of the month to be reviewed** (HF Policy
3.9(b)).
