# Biweekly Tasks

Every two weeks, the FO clears two approval queues: payroll in Payworks, and
expenses and payments in Zoho Expenses and Plooto. Both are on the same
cadence, so do them together.

---

## Payroll Review

Every pay period, the FO reviews and approves employee time off requests and
employee timesheets in Payworks.

Full procedures are documented in the Finance & Accounting Guide:
[**Payroll — Payworks Review**](https://ubcgss.github.io/finance_accounting_guide/payroll/)

---

## Credit Card and Plooto Approvals (Zoho Expenses)

GSS corporate credit cards are held by the General Manager ($10,000 limit),
Events Manager ($10,000 limit), and Administrative Assistant ($10,000 limit).
Reimbursements and vendor payments go through the same Zoho Expenses queue
and are paid through Plooto.

Every two weeks, review and approve everything waiting for you in Zoho
Expenses, then approve the resulting payments in Plooto.

**Credit card expenses:**

| Step | Who | Deadline |
|------|-----|----------|
| 1. Cardholder uploads expenses with supporting documentation | Cardholder | Within 3 business days of statement receipt |
| 2. Department Head reviews and approves | Department Head | Before the next biweekly FO review |
| 3. FO reviews budget code, amount, purpose | FO | At the biweekly review |
| 4. Bookkeeper enters approved expenses into Zoho Books | Bookkeeper | After FO approval |

**Reimbursements and vendor payments (Plooto):**

| Step | Who |
|------|-----|
| 1. Expense entered into Zoho Expenses with supporting documents | GM or relevant employee |
| 2. Approvals | President and VP UAA |
| 3. Final approval | FO |
| 4. Payment executed via Plooto (e-transfer / EFT) | GM, with FO approval of the payment in Plooto |
| 5. Payment recorded in Zoho Books | Bookkeeper, in the first week of the following month |

As FO, review each transaction for:
- Correct budget code (budget line)
- Reasonable amount relative to budget
- Business purpose documented
- Supporting documentation (receipt, invoice) attached

If a transaction is unusual, reconcile directly with the cardholder or
requester. Suspicious transactions may be reported to the HFC. You may approve
the expense or seek clarification by requesting relevant supporting
documentation.

{: .note }
Cardholders who do not reconcile will be sent a reminder. Continued failure
may result in loss of access to the corporate credit card.

See [Zoho](../../systems/zoho/) and [Banking](../../systems/banking/) for
system details.
