Union. Banking and credit cards are **two separate systems**: separate websites,
logins, and support lines.

(Pinetree branch). She covers both banking and credit cards.

### Online Banking and Access Cards

Signers (FO, President, VP UAA) each hold a Vancity access card and their own
online banking login, which they use to approve transfers and payments. The GSS
no longer issues debit cards. See
[Changing Signing Authority](../../transition/accounts-access/#changing-signing-authority)
for how to update signers at turnover.

### Corporate Credit Cards

Each card has a $10,000 limit:

| Cardholder | Used by |
|------------|---------|
| General Manager | GM |
| Events Manager | Events Manager |
| Administrative Assistant | Administrative Assistant |
| Financial Officer | FSI Coordinator |

- **Paying the cards:** the card balances are paid from the GSS bank account
  through the **banking** site, not the credit card site.

- **Statements:** the FO downloads card statements from the credit card site and

- **Lost or stolen card, limit changes, adding or removing cardholders:** call
  the business credit card line (604-676-8431) or contact the account manager.
  See [Credit Cards](../../transition/accounts-access/#credit-cards) for the
  turnover process.

### Payment Method Rules

For Interac e-transfers, payments can be made through Vancity, but they require
approval/signing by two executives or signing officers before release.

For EFT payments, use Plooto. As of 2025, EFTs cannot be completed directly
through Vancity online; Vancity EFTs require going to the branch. Confirm the
current process with the GM before initiating any EFT outside Plooto.

## Plooto (Payment Platform)

Plooto is the GSS's online payment software platform. After expenses are
approved in Zoho Expenses, the GM executes e-transfers through Plooto. The
Bookkeeper enters payment notifications and confirmations into the accounting
system in the first week of the subsequent month.

## Payworks (Payroll and Timesheets)

**Purpose:** Timesheet tracking and payroll processing.

**Payroll schedule:** Semi-monthly — 24 payrolls per year.
- Pay period 1st–15th: pay date is the **22nd** of the same month.
- Pay period 16th–31st: pay date is the **7th** of the next month.
- If a pay date falls on a weekend or holiday, payment is made ahead of
  schedule. Exception: the last payroll of the calendar year (Dec 16–31) has
  a pay date of Dec 31; no payment on Jan 7.

**Workflow:**
1. Bickert Management (Bookkeeper) sends GSS Executives and staff a task
   requesting payroll updates via Zoho Projects.
2. Timesheets are uploaded to Payworks semi-monthly.
3. GSS approves Pay Run by 2:30 PM.
4. Bickert Management finalizes the Pay Run; payroll is released.

**Employee types:**
- **Salary employees:** GSS executives, full-time staff, Health and Dental
  Coordinator. Paid annual salary; not eligible for overtime unless specifically
  approved by the GM and HR Committee.
- **Hourly employees:** Part-time staff, all Work Learn student positions.

**FO role:** New hires and terminations must be signed off by the President
and FO.
