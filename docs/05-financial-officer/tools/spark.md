# Spark (Email)

[Spark](https://sparkmailapp.com/) is an email app that helps simplify email
processing. This is a key tool given the volume of email the FO receives each
day. The app is available for Mac, Windows, iOS, and Android. The FO uses it for
the finance inbox, **`finance@gss.ubc.ca`**.

## Why Spark

- **Fast sorting with keyboard shortcuts.** You can archive, move to a folder,
  snooze, or pin without touching the mouse, so clearing the inbox goes quickly.
  The full list is under Spark's settings (Shortcuts).
- **Reminders.** Snoozing is useful to clear your inbox from email you will only
  need later (e.g. snoozing the Council agenda for the day of Council), while
  reminders help to keep in mind emails you should follow up on.
- **Scheduled send.** Write a reply now and choose when it goes out (Send
  Later). For example, you can keep late-night replies to business hours, or
  time an email to reach someone's inbox first thing in the morning.

---

## Working Practices

1. **Work through the inbox once or twice a day**, not continuously. Each email
   gets one of three outcomes: reply now, snooze, or file to a folder.
2. **File anything that needs no further action** into its folder (see below),
   so the inbox only holds open items.
3. **Use reminders instead of leaving emails unread** as a to-do marker. For
   follow-ups that aren't tied to one email, use [FollowUpThen](../followupthen/).
4. **Use scheduled send** for anything that should land at a specific time.

## Pre-drafted Email Links

Some guide pages link to a pre-filled email (for example the monthly
[CRA notices email](../../regular-tasks/monthly/#download-and-send-cra-notices-to-accountant)).
These links start with `readdle-spark://compose`, so they open a new message
in Spark even when another app, such as Mail, is the Mac's default for
`mailto:` links. Spark sends from its default account, so keep
`finance@gss.ubc.ca` as the default sending account, and check the From line
before sending. The links fill in the recipient, subject, and body only;
attachments are added by hand.

## Using Spark with Claude

Claude can read the finance inbox through Spark to summarize long threads,
build a timeline of what was agreed, and draft replies that open in Spark for
you to review and send. A daily triage routine also uses Spark to produce the
morning inbox digest. See [Claude](../claude/#reviewing-long-email-threads-in-spark).
