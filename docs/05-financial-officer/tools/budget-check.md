# Budget Integrity Check

The **Budget Integrity Check** tool and full user documentation live in the
[GSS Finance & Accounting Guide](https://ubcgss.github.io/finance_accounting_guide/tools/budget-check/),
where finance staff and the Accountant maintain and run it alongside the FO.

[Open Budget Check (Finance & Accounting Guide)](https://ubcgss.github.io/finance_accounting_guide/assets/budget-check/){: .btn .btn-primary }
[Read Full Instructions & Checks](https://ubcgss.github.io/finance_accounting_guide/tools/budget-check/){: .btn }

## When the FO Uses It

- Before the final master budget goes to HFC for approval (May).
- After every revision of the master during the year, such as a correction or a
  reallocation.
- When taking over as FO, to check what you are inheriting.

The full FO review checklist, including the parts the automated check cannot
verify, is in [Budget Integrity Review](../../governance/budget-process/#budget-integrity-review).
